# Tokenomics Due Diligence

The goal is to understand who owns the token, how supply changes, how value flows and what incentives exist.

## Core Metrics

Check:

- Total supply
- Circulating supply
- Market capitalization
- Fully diluted valuation (FDV)
- Token utility
- Inflation and emissions

## Allocation

Identify allocations to:

- Team
- Investors
- Treasury
- Community/ecosystem
- Liquidity
- Other disclosed categories

## Vesting and Unlocks

Review:

- Team unlock schedule
- Investor unlock schedule
- Vesting terms
- Large upcoming unlocks
- Emissions

Large insider/team unlocks with weak vesting are a red flag identified in the source material.

## Holder Concentration

Do not look only at the largest wallets. Determine whether large wallets are:

- Exchanges
- Treasury wallets
- Liquidity pools
- Vesting contracts
- Market makers
- Team wallets
- Insiders
- Related wallets

The key question is: **Who owns the tokens?**

## Economic Questions

- Why does the token exist?
- How does value flow through the ecosystem?
- What creates demand or utility?
- What incentives are being used?
- What happens to the economics under increased selling pressure?

## Red Flags

The source highlights:

- Highly concentrated token supply
- Unrealistic APY or guaranteed returns
- No clear token utility
- Large insider/team unlocks with weak vesting
